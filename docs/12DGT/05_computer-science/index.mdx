### [Computer Security](computer-security/README.mdx)

Built around one very exposed fictional robotics club — one shared password, a laptop that goes home in a backpack, and no backup plan. Covers the CIA triad, malware and social engineering, access control, encryption and hashing, networks, backups, and how to design a real security plan.

</SectionCard>

### [Encryption](encryption/README.mdx)

Built around real companies and real technologies instead of a case study — Apple, Google, WhatsApp, banks, garage door remotes. Covers symmetric and public-key encryption, HTTPS, hashing and passwords, everyday uses, human factors and ethics, and future-proofing against quantum computing.
