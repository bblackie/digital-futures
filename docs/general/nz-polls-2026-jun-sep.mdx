# NZ Polling Tracker — June to September 2026

Party vote share from every published poll, June through early September 2026,
covering the run-in to the 7 November 2026 general election. Hover the chart for
exact figures at each poll; click a party name in the legend to hide or show its line.

The Post/Freshwater Strategy's 5–11 June poll is omitted — that house doesn't ask
about the Opportunity Party, so it can't sit cleanly on the same axes as the rest.

**House effects to keep in mind:** Roy Morgan and Talbot Mills consistently show
Labour several points apart from each other regardless of when the poll ran — that's
a systematic house difference, not the electorate swinging. Opportunity's rise
(3–4% in June to 8–9.5% in August) is the one trend that shows up consistently
across every house, which is why it reads as a real shift rather than noise.
